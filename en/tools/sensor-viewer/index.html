About Sensor Viewer

Visualize gyroscope, accelerometer, and compass readings on supported mobile devices. Processing happens locally in your browser, and the tool does not upload the entered content.

Privacy and compatibility

Results depend on browser capabilities and the supplied input. Review generated output before using it in production or formal documents.
